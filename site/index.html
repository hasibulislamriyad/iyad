Questions

Does Notch send my data anywhere?

No. Notes and clipboard history are saved on your own computer, and the app makes no network requests.

Will it get in the way of games?

It hides itself when a fullscreen app is in front, and you can switch that off in Settings.

Does it cost anything?

It’s free during the beta. If that changes, early users will hear first.

What about Mac or Linux?

Windows first. Other systems come later.
